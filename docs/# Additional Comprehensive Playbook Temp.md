### Playbook 16: Entryway Deep Clean - First Impressions Matter

**Category:** Cleaning

**Description:** Your entryway sets the tone for your entire home. This playbook helps you create a welcoming, organized space that makes coming home feel good and leaving feel less chaotic.

**Total Estimated Time:** 1h 30m

#### Steps:

**Step 1: Gather Entryway Supplies**
- All-purpose cleaner, glass cleaner, dusting cloths, vacuum, mop, organizing bins, trash bag
- **Estimated time:** 5 minutes

**Step 2: Complete Declutter Blitz**
- Remove everything that doesn't belong in the entryway
- Sort items: keep here, relocate to another room, donate, discard
- Be honest about what you actually use daily
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes

**Step 3: Shoe & Coat Organization**
- Remove all shoes from area
- Clean shoe storage rack/area
- Return only regularly-worn shoes
- Organize coats by season and frequency of use
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 4: Wall & Switch Wipe-Down**
- Wipe down walls around light switches and door frames
- These high-touch areas get surprisingly dirty
- Remove any scuff marks or fingerprints
- Clean light switches thoroughly
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 5: Door Deep Clean**
- Wipe down front door interior and exterior
- Clean doorknob/handle until it shines
- Don't forget the area around the lock
- Clean door frame and threshold
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 6: Window & Glass Treatment**
- Clean any windows or glass in/near door
- Wipe down window sills and tracks
- Clean any glass in picture frames or decor
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 7: Mirror Magic**
- Clean entryway mirror streak-free
- Wipe down mirror frame
- A clean mirror makes the space feel bigger
- **Estimated time:** 5 minutes

**Step 8: Furniture & Surface Care**
- Wipe down console table or entry bench
- Clean any seating areas
- Clean cushions or upholstery as needed
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 9: Decorative Items Dust**
- Dust all vases, knick-knacks, picture frames
- Clean and polish any decorative items
- Less is more - remove excess decor
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 10: Storage Unit Clean-Out**
- Empty shoe racks completely
- Wipe down with all-purpose cleaner
- Clean inside of any storage bins or cabinets
- Organize items as you return them
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 11: Floor & Mat Refresh**
- Shake out or vacuum entry mats/rugs outside
- Sweep or vacuum floor thoroughly
- Get into corners and under furniture
- Mop floor with appropriate cleaner
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 12: Air Vent & Light Fixture Care**
- Remove and clean air vent covers
- Dust and clean light fixtures
- Replace any burnt-out bulbs
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 13: Umbrella Stand & Accessories**
- Empty and clean umbrella stand
- Organize umbrellas (discard broken ones)
- Clean any hooks, key holders, or mail organizers
- **Estimated time:** 5 minutes

**Step 14: Final Organization & Styling**
- Return only essential, beautiful items
- Ensure everyone in household has designated space for their items
- Add a welcoming touch: fresh flowers, seasonal decor
- Step back and admire your welcoming entryway
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

### Playbook 17: Garage Deep Clean - Reclaim Your Space

**Category:** Cleaning

**Description:** Transform your garage from chaotic storage nightmare to organized, functional space. This comprehensive playbook tackles the unique challenges of garage cleaning while keeping you focused and preventing overwhelm.

**Total Estimated Time:** 4h 0m

#### Steps:

**Step 1: Prep & Safety Check**
- Wear work gloves and appropriate clothing
- Gather: heavy-duty trash bags, broom, vacuum, cleaning supplies, storage bins, labels
- Open garage door for ventilation
- Hydrate and set realistic expectations
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 2: The Great Emptying**
- Remove ALL items from garage (yes, everything)
- Place items on driveway or lawn in categories
- This seems overwhelming but is essential for a true reset
- Work in sections if needed for very full garages
- **Estimated time:** 45 minutes

**Step 3: Ruthless Sorting**
- Create piles: Keep, Donate, Sell, Recycle, Trash
- Be honest about what you actually use
- Broken tools? Trash. Duplicate items? Choose the best one.
- Items you haven't touched in 2+ years? Let them go.
- **Estimated time:** 40 minutes

**Step 4: Ceiling & Wall Attack**
- Dust ceiling fixtures and remove cobwebs
- Wipe down walls with damp cloth
- Pay attention to areas with oil stains or marks
- Clean light fixtures and replace bulbs if needed
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes

**Step 5: Window & Door Cleaning**
- Clean garage windows inside and out
- Wipe down window sills and tracks
- Clean garage door interior and exterior
- Wipe down regular entry door if applicable
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 6: Shelving & Storage Deep Clean**
- Remove all items from shelves (if you didn't already)
- Wipe down all shelf surfaces thoroughly
- Clean inside cabinets if you have them
- Repair any wobbly or damaged shelving
- **Estimated time:** 25 minutes

**Step 7: Floor Sweep & Debris Removal**
- Sweep entire floor thoroughly
- Remove all dirt, dust, and debris
- Get into all corners and edges
- Use vacuum for finer dust and dirt
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes

**Step 8: Stain Treatment**
- Apply degreaser to oil stains or spills
- Scrub with stiff brush
- Rinse with water (use hose if available)
- Allow to dry before replacing items
- **Estimated time:** 30 minutes

**Step 9: Workbench Reset**
- Clear off workbench completely
- Wipe down with all-purpose cleaner
- Organize workbench drawers if applicable
- Set up tool organization system
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes

**Step 10: Tool Cleaning & Organization**
- Wipe down all tools with damp cloth
- Apply rust prevention if needed
- Organize tools by type and frequency of use
- Hang frequently used tools for easy access
- Store seasonal tools together
- **Estimated time:** 25 minutes

**Step 11: Lawn Equipment Maintenance**
- Clean lawn mower, trimmer, leaf blower
- Check gas levels and remove old gas if needed
- Wipe down bikes and outdoor sports equipment
- Organize seasonal equipment together
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes

**Step 12: Storage Bin Organization**
- Place "Keep" items into clear storage bins
- Label ALL bins clearly and specifically
- Use bins for categories: holiday decor, camping, sports, etc.
- Stack bins efficiently on shelves
- Keep frequently used items at eye level
- **Estimated time:** 35 minutes

**Step 13: Trash & Recycling Area**
- Clean trash and recycling bins thoroughly
- Disinfect interiors and exteriors
- Designate specific area for bins
- Ensure bins are easily accessible
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 14: Final Placement & Optimization**
- Return items to garage in organized fashion
- Place frequently used items near door
- Keep floor space as clear as possible
- Ensure car(s) can fit properly
- Create zones: tool zone, sports zone, seasonal zone
- Label shelves and areas for easy maintenance
- **Estimated time:** 30 minutes

**Step 15: Celebrate & Plan Maintenance**
- Take before/after photos for motivation
- Set calendar reminder for quarterly garage check
- Admire your transformation
- **Estimated time:** 5 minutes

**Total Estimated Time:** 2h 30m

#### Steps:

**Step 1: Tupperware Takeover**
- Empty all food storage containers
- Match lids to containers - discard orphans
- Stack and organize by size
- Keep only what you actually use
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes

**Step 2: Dish Purge**
- Remove all dishes from cabinets
- Discard cracked or chipped items
- Donate mismatched sets you don't love
- Keep service for number of people you actually host
- **Estimated time:** 25 minutes

**Step 3: Glass & Mug Reduction**
- Empty glass/mug cabinet
- Discard chipped glasses
- Keep favorite mugs only (not every promotional mug ever received)
- Donate excess to thrift store
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 4: Gadget Reality Check**
- Remove all kitchen gadgets and tools
- When did you last use that avocado slicer? Be honest.
- Keep versatile tools, remove single-use items
- Donate duplicates (do you really need 3 can openers?)
- **Estimated time:** 25 minutes

**Step 5: Pantry Expiration Expedition**
- Check ALL expiration dates
- Discard expired goods immediately
- Group similar items together
- Create "use soon" section for near-expiration items
- **Estimated time:** 30 minutes

**Step 6: Spice Rack Refresh**
- Check spice expiration dates (they do expire!)
- Discard dried-out or flavorless spices
- Organize alphabetically or by cuisine type
- Note what needs replacing
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 7: Under-Sink Organization**
- Remove everything from under sink
- Discard mostly-empty cleaning products
- Check for old sponges and scrubbers
- Organize remaining items in bins or caddies
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes

**Step 8: Appliance Assessment**
- Review all small appliances
- Be honest about that bread maker you used once
- Keep appliances you use monthly or more
- Store or donate rarely-used items
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 9: Utensil Drawer Overhaul**
- Empty utensil drawer completely
- Discard broken items and duplicates
- Keep one good version of each tool type
- Use drawer dividers for organization
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 10: Final Organization & Cleaning**
- Wipe down all empty cabinets and drawers
- Return only kept items in organized fashion
- Label pantry sections if helpful
- Admire your functional, decluttered kitchen
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes

### Playbook 22: Closet Declutter - Wardrobe Reset

**Category:** Cleaning

**Description:** Transform your closet from overwhelming chaos to curated collection. This systematic approach helps you identify what you truly wear and love, making getting dressed easier every day.

**Total Estimated Time:** 2h 0m

#### Steps:

**Step 1: The Great Removal**
- Take EVERYTHING out of closet
- Yes, everything - this is essential
- Place on bed or clean floor area
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 2: The Honest Try-On**
- Try on anything you're uncertain about
- If it doesn't fit or make you feel good, it goes
- Create piles: Keep, Donate, Sell, Repair, Trash
- **Estimated time:** 45 minutes

**Step 3: The One-Year Rule**
- If you haven't worn it in a year (exceptions for formal wear), let it go
- Be honest - you're not saving it "just in case"
- That "someday when I lose weight" pile? Box it and date it. If you hit that goal, celebrate with new clothes.
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 4: Damage Assessment**
- Examine kept items for damage
- Stained, ripped, or pilled items: trash or repair pile
- Be realistic about what you'll actually repair
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 5: Seasonal Sort**
- Separate by season if you have storage space
- Out-of-season items can be stored in bins
- This reduces closet overwhelm
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 6: Closet Deep Clean**
- Vacuum or sweep empty closet floor
- Wipe down shelves and rods
- Clean any closet organizers
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 7: Strategic Return**
- Organize by category (pants, shirts, dresses)
- Then by color within categories
- Hang items facing same direction
- Use matching hangers if possible (game-changer for visual calm)
- **Estimated time:** 25 minutes

**Step 8: Shoe Edit**
- Apply same rules to shoes
- Worn out? Uncomfortable? Haven't worn in a year? Out.
- Organize remaining shoes by type or frequency of use
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 9: Accessory Organization**
- Sort through scarves, belts, bags
- Keep only loved and used items
- Organize in a way that makes them visible and accessible
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 10: Donation Box Prep**
- Box up donation items immediately
- Put box in car trunk today
- Schedule donation drop-off on calendar
- Don't let it sit - you'll second-guess yourself
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

### Playbook 23: Paper Avalanche Control

**Category:** Cleaning

**Description:** Tackle the paper clutter that accumulates everywhere. From mail piles to old documents, this playbook helps you establish a paper management system that actually works.

**Total Estimated Time:** 2h 0m

#### Steps:

**Step 1: Gather All Paper**
- Collect paper from every room
- Counters, drawers, "junk drawer," car, everywhere
- Create one central sorting station
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 2: Create Sorting System**
- Set up labeled piles: File, Action Needed, Shred, Recycle, Scan
- Get folders ready for filing
- Position shredder and recycle bin nearby
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 3: Mail Mountain**
- Sort all mail first - it's usually most of the pile
- Recycle junk mail immediately
- Shred anything with personal info
- File important documents
- **Estimated time:** 25 minutes

**Step 4: Old Bills & Statements**
- Check retention requirements for your situation
- Generally: tax-related keep 7 years, other bills 1 year
- Shred old financial documents
- Consider going paperless for future
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes

**Step 5: Medical & Insurance Papers**
- Create medical file with current info
- Shred old insurance cards and outdated medical records
- Keep: current insurance cards, recent medical summaries
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 6: Receipts & Warranties**
- Keep only receipts for: taxes, warranties still valid, returns you might make
- Most stores can reprint receipts if needed
- File warranties with product manuals
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 7: Kids' Papers & Artwork**
- Keep only meaningful pieces
- Take photos of artwork before recycling
- Create memory box for truly special items
- Don't keep every worksheet
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes

**Step 8: Manuals & Instructions**
- Most are available online now
- Keep only for items you actually use and can't find online
- Recycle the rest
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 9: Magazine & Catalog Purge**
- Keep only current month's magazines
- Tear out any recipes/articles you want, recycle rest
- Cancel catalog subscriptions you don't want
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 10: Filing & Shredding**
- File all "keep" papers in organized system
- Shred all sensitive documents
- Recycle all non-sensitive paper
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes

**Step 11: System Setup for Future**
- Create daily mail sorting spot
- Set up action file for items needing attention
- Schedule weekly 10-minute paper processing
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

### Playbook 26: Laundry Day System

**Category:** Cleaning

**Description:** Transform laundry from an overwhelming mountain to a manageable system. This playbook breaks down the entire laundry process with timing that works with your actual life.

**Total Estimated Time:** 2h 30m (spread throughout day with breaks)

#### Steps:

**Step 1: Gather & Sort**
- Collect all dirty laundry from hampers
- Sort by color and fabric type
- Whites, darks, colors, delicates
- Check pockets!
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 2: Pre-Treat Stains**
- Check all items for stains
- Apply stain remover to problem areas
- Let sit while you prep first load
- **Estimated time:** 5 minutes

**Step 3: Load #1 - Whites/Lights**
- Load washer without overfilling
- Add detergent (measure - more isn't better)
- Start cycle
- Set timer for when it will be done
- **Estimated time:** 5 minutes

**Step 4: Break Time**
- Do something else while washer runs
- This isn't wasted time - it's built-in breaks
- **Estimated time:** 30-45 minutes

**Step 5: Transfer to Dryer**
- Move wet clothes to dryer
- Clean lint trap (every time!)
- Set appropriate heat level
- Start dryer
- **Estimated time:** 5 minutes

**Step 6: Load #2 Start**
- Load next batch in washer
- Start cycle
- Set timer
- **Estimated time:** 5 minutes

**Step 7: Break Time #2**
- Continue with your day
- Set phone reminder for dryer completion
- **Estimated time:** Variable

**Step 8: Fold Load #1 Immediately**
- As soon as dryer stops, fold clothes
- Folding immediately prevents wrinkles and overwhelming piles
- Put folded items in basket by person/room
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 9: Continue Cycle**
- Move load 2 to dryer
- Start load 3 if needed
- Repeat process until all laundry is complete
- **Estimated time:** Variable

**Step 10: Put Away Session**
- Put away all folded laundry
- Don't leave baskets sitting - that's how the system breaks down
- Everything in its proper place
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes

**Step 11: Sheet Day** (if applicable)
- Strip beds and wash sheets
- Follow same process
- Make beds with fresh sheets immediately when dry
- **Estimated time:** 30 minutes additional

**Step 12: Laundry Room Reset**
- Wipe down washer and dryer
- Organize laundry supplies
- Empty lint trap one more time
- Sweep floor
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes
