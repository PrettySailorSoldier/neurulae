# Custom Playbook Templates for Neurulae

## Playbook Template 1: The Complete House Reset - Reclaim Your Space

**Category:** Whole Home

**Description:** A compassionate, room-by-room deep clean designed specifically for coming out of a difficult period. This playbook breaks down the overwhelming task of getting your entire house back in order into manageable chunks. You don't have to do it all at once - tackle one room per day, or even one step at a time. Progress, not perfection.

**Total Estimated Time:** 12h 0m (Spread over multiple days - do NOT attempt in one day!)

**Recommended Approach:** Choose ONE room per day. Rest between rooms. Celebrate each completed room.

---

### Steps:

#### **Step 1: Gather Your Reset Arsenal**
**Description:**
Before you start any room, gather all your supplies in one place. This prevents the exhausting mid-task hunt for supplies that can derail your momentum.

**What to gather:**
- Trash bags (multiple - you'll need them)
- Laundry baskets or bins for sorting
- All-purpose cleaner
- Disinfecting wipes or spray
- Glass cleaner
- Microfiber cloths or paper towels
- Vacuum
- Broom and mop
- Comfortable clothes you can move in
- Water bottle (stay hydrated!)
- Phone with timer/music

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

---

#### **Step 2: Master Bedroom - Your Sanctuary First**
**Description:**
Start with your bedroom because you deserve a peaceful place to rest. A clean bedroom helps with sleep, which helps with everything else. You're not just cleaning a room; you're reclaiming your sanctuary.

**Tasks:**
1. **Strip the bed completely** - All sheets, pillowcases, mattress cover. Start a load of laundry immediately.
2. **Clothes decision time** - Dirty clothes → hamper. Clean clothes → put away or donate. The "maybe" pile → make a decision. You've got this.
3. **Surface clear** - Nightstands, dresser top. Everything gets a home or goes.
4. **Dust all surfaces** - Top to bottom. Don't worry about perfect, just better.
5. **Vacuum/sweep floor thoroughly** - Under the bed if you can access it.
6. **Make bed with fresh sheets** - This is your reward. Tonight you sleep in a fresh, clean bed.

**Estimated time:** 1h 30m

---

#### **Step 3: Office - Command Center Restoration**
**Description:**
Your office affects your productivity and mental clarity. A clear space supports a clearer mind. Take this one surface at a time.

**Tasks:**
1. **Desk surface intervention** - Everything off the desk. Wipe it down. Only essentials go back on.
2. **Paper purge** - Trash, recycle, or file. If you haven't looked at it in months, you probably don't need it.
3. **Organize supplies** - Pens that work, supplies you use. Everything else goes.
4. **Tech clean** - Wipe down keyboard, mouse, monitor, desk phone.
5. **Floor clear and clean** - Vacuum or sweep, then mop if hard floor.
6. **Cable management** - Just get them off the floor. Perfection not required.

**Estimated time:** 1h 15m

---

#### **Step 4: Bathroom 1 (Main) - Daily Reset Space**
**Description:**
A clean bathroom makes daily hygiene routines less overwhelming. This is self-care infrastructure.

**Tasks:**
1. **Clear all surfaces** - Counters, tub edge, toilet tank. Everything comes off.
2. **Throw away expired products** - Check dates. Be ruthless. Expired = trash.
3. **Scrub toilet completely** - Bowl, seat, base, behind. Use good cleaner and let it work.
4. **Clean sink and counter** - Scrub sink, wipe counter, polish faucet.
5. **Shower/tub deep clean** - Spray, let sit 5 minutes, scrub, rinse thoroughly.
6. **Mirror and glass** - Make it shine. Seeing yourself clearly matters.
7. **Floor** - Sweep/vacuum, then mop. Get in the corners.
8. **Fresh towels** - Clean towels only. Wash the old ones.

**Estimated time:** 1h 0m

---

#### **Step 5: Bathroom 2 - Second Sanctuary**
**Description:**
Same process, fresh space. You're getting good at this.

**Tasks:**
1. **Clear surfaces and dispose expired items**
2. **Toilet deep clean**
3. **Sink and counter scrub**
4. **Shower/tub deep clean**
5. **Mirror and glass shine**
6. **Floor sweep and mop**
7. **Fresh towels**

**Estimated time:** 1h 0m

---

#### **Step 6: Living Room - Family Space Revival**
**Description:**
This is where you live. Where you rest, where you gather. Reclaim this space for joy, not just existing.

**Tasks:**
1. **Declutter sweep** - Walk through with trash bag. Anything that's trash → bag.
2. **Surface by surface** - Coffee table, side tables, entertainment center. Clear, wipe, return only what belongs.
3. **Couch/furniture care** - Remove cushions, vacuum underneath and in crevices. Vacuum upholstery.
4. **Dust everything** - Shelves, TV, picture frames, decorations.
5. **Floor comprehensive clean** - Vacuum thoroughly (including under furniture if possible), or sweep and mop.
6. **Finishing touches** - Fluff pillows, fold throws, arrange remote controls in one spot.

**Estimated time:** 1h 30m

---

#### **Step 7: Kitchen - Heart of the Home Reset**
**Description:**
The kitchen affects everything - your nutrition, your energy, your mood. A clean kitchen makes feeding yourself easier. This matters.

**Tasks:**
1. **Dish intervention** - ALL dishes get washed, dried, put away. Start dishwasher if you have one. This is non-negotiable.
2. **Counter clear and clean** - Remove everything. Wipe down completely. Only daily-use items return.
3. **Appliance care** - Microwave inside and out, stovetop, coffee maker exterior. Just the outside is fine for now.
4. **Sink deep clean** - Scrub sink, polish faucet, clean around faucet base.
5. **Fridge quick sort** - Remove obvious expired items. Wipe up spills you can see. Deep clean can wait.
6. **Trash and recycling** - Empty both. Put in fresh bags. Take all trash outside.
7. **Floor thorough clean** - Sweep, then mop. Get the corners and under the table.
8. **Dish towel refresh** - Clean dish towels only.

**Estimated time:** 2h 0m

---

#### **Step 8: Dining Room 1 (Main) - Gathering Space**
**Description:**
Whether you eat here or not, this space sets a tone. Clear and clean.

**Tasks:**
1. **Table complete clear** - Everything off the table. Every. Thing.
2. **Table deep clean** - Wipe down thoroughly. Polish if wood.
3. **Chairs attention** - Wipe down all chairs, legs included.
4. **Clear other surfaces** - Buffet, sideboard, shelves. Wipe down.
5. **Dust and detail** - Light fixture, artwork, any decor.
6. **Floor care** - Sweep or vacuum, then mop.

**Estimated time:** 45m

---

#### **Step 9: Dining Room 2 - Second Space**
**Description:**
Same energy, different room. You've got a system now.

**Tasks:**
1. **Complete table clear and clean**
2. **All chairs wiped down**
3. **Other surfaces cleared and cleaned**
4. **Dust and detail work**
5. **Floor sweep/vacuum and mop**

**Estimated time:** 45m

---

#### **Step 10: Sunroom - Light and Life Space**
**Description:**
This space is meant for light, for life, for peace. Let's make it that again.

**Tasks:**
1. **Declutter completely** - If it doesn't belong in a sunroom, it goes elsewhere.
2. **Windows are priority** - Inside and out if possible. This space is about light.
3. **Furniture care** - Wipe down all surfaces, vacuum any upholstered pieces.
4. **Plant care if applicable** - Water, remove dead leaves, wipe dust off leaves.
5. **Floor thorough clean** - Sweep or vacuum, mop if applicable.
6. **Create a peaceful vibe** - Arrange furniture for comfort, not just storage.

**Estimated time:** 1h 0m

---

#### **Step 11: Bedroom (Second) - Guest or Personal Space**
**Description:**
Whether this is a guest room or another personal space, it deserves the same care.

**Tasks:**
1. **Strip bed and start laundry**
2. **Declutter surfaces and floor**
3. **Dust all surfaces top to bottom**
4. **Closet quick sort** (if applicable)
5. **Vacuum or sweep floor**
6. **Make bed with fresh linens**

**Estimated time:** 1h 15m

---

#### **Step 12: The Final Walk-Through - Acknowledge Your Victory**
**Description:**
Walk through your entire home. See what you've accomplished. This isn't about what's left - it's about honoring what you've done.

**Tasks:**
1. **Walk each room slowly**
2. **Notice the difference** - Really see it
3. **Take one "after" photo of each space** - For tough days ahead, you'll have proof you can do hard things
4. **Do one small thing you notice** - But just one. You're done.
5. **Rest** - Seriously. You did something huge.

**Estimated time:** 20m

---

### Important Reminders:

- **Pace yourself:** One room per day is a victory. Two rooms in a week is excellent. The whole house in a month is incredible.
- **Rest between rooms:** You're recovering, not competing.
- **Good enough is perfect:** Done is better than perfect.
- **Celebrate each room:** Seriously. Each room is a win.
- **Ask for help if you can:** This is a lot. Help is not weakness.

## Playbook Template 2: Emergency Clean - Low Energy Maintenance Mode

**Category:** Whole Home

**Description:** For the days when you have minimal energy but need your space to be safe, clean, and sanitary. This is your "bare minimum viable environment" routine - the essentials that keep depression from getting worse by maintaining basic hygiene in your space. Low energy? This has your back.

**Total Estimated Time:** 1h 30m

**Designed for:** Low energy days, maintenance between deeper cleans, when you're functioning but barely.

---

### Steps:

#### **Step 1: The 5-Minute Kitchen Save**
**Description:**
The kitchen affects everything. Keep it barely functional, and you can feed yourself. That matters.

**Bare minimum tasks:**
- Put ALL dirty dishes in dishwasher or stack neatly in sink (don't wash, just contain)
- Wipe down counters with disinfecting wipes (just the visible surfaces)
- Take out kitchen trash if it's full or smells
- Quick sweep of floor (just visible crumbs/debris)
- That's it. Kitchen is functional enough.

**Estimated time:** 5m

---

#### **Step 2: Bathroom Rapid Sanitize**
**Description:**
Hygiene basics. This keeps you healthy even when you can't do more.

**Bare minimum tasks:**
- Toilet: Quick scrub of bowl, wipe seat and handle with disinfecting wipe
- Sink: Wipe down sink and counter with disinfecting wipe
- Mirror: Quick wipe if you can see toothpaste splatter (optional)
- Floor: Kick towels/clothes into hamper, quick sweep if needed
- Fresh hand towel if the current one is gross

**Do this for BOTH bathrooms.**

**Estimated time:** 10m (5m each bathroom)

---

#### **Step 3: The Bedroom Minimum**
**Description:**
You need a place to rest. Make the bed. That's the anchor.

**Bare minimum tasks:**
- Make the bed (pull up covers, arrange pillows - it doesn't have to be perfect)
- Dirty clothes → hamper (just pick them up off the floor)
- Put water glass on nightstand (you need to hydrate)
- Open blinds/curtains (light helps)

**Estimated time:** 5m

---

#### **Step 4: Living Room Surface Reset**
**Description:**
Just the surfaces. Just what's visible. That's all we're doing.

**Bare minimum tasks:**
- Collect any dishes/cups → take to kitchen
- Grab obvious trash → throw away
- Stack any clutter neatly (don't organize, just stack)
- Fluff couch pillows (visual order helps mood)
- Quick surface wipe of coffee table if sticky

**Estimated time:** 5m

---

#### **Step 5: Critical Disinfection Points**
**Description:**
The things you touch constantly. These spread germs and affect your health. Quick wipe = big impact.

**High-touch surfaces to disinfect:**
- All door handles in the house
- Light switches (especially bedroom, bathroom, kitchen)
- TV remote controls
- Phone (wipe it down with electronics-safe wipe)
- Faucet handles (bathroom and kitchen)
- Refrigerator handle
- Microwave handle

**Use disinfecting wipes. One wipe per room is fine. This isn't about perfect, it's about reducing germs.**

**Estimated time:** 10m

---

#### **Step 6: Floor Spot Clean - High Traffic Only**
**Description:**
Not the whole floor. Just the paths you walk. Just the visible dirt.

**Quick floor maintenance:**
- Kitchen: Quick sweep of walkway and in front of sink
- Bathroom(s): Quick sweep if you see hair/dust
- Living room: Vacuum or sweep the main walkway only
- Bedroom: Quick pickup of visible items, optional quick vacuum of path

**Not the edges. Not under furniture. Just where you walk.**

**Estimated time:** 15m

---

#### **Step 7: The Essential Trash Run**
**Description:**
Trash accumulates fast during depression. Get it out of your space.

**Trash task:**
- Empty all visible trash cans (bathroom, bedroom, kitchen, office)
- Grab any obvious trash from surfaces (food wrappers, packaging, etc.)
- Take all trash bags to outside bin
- Put new bags in trash cans
- This one task improves air quality immediately

**Estimated time:** 10m

---

#### **Step 8: Air Quality Quick Fix**
**Description:**
Fresh air and better smell = mood lift. This is free therapy.

**Quick air improvements:**
- Open windows for 10 minutes if weather allows (set a timer)
- Turn on bathroom fans while you're doing other tasks
- If you have air purifier, turn it on
- Optional: Quick spray of air freshener ONLY if space is already clean (don't spray over dirt)

**Estimated time:** 2m (plus 10m passive window time)

---

#### **Step 9: Laundry Minimum Viable Action**
**Description:**
You need clean clothes. This is the smallest possible action toward that goal.

**Minimum laundry action (choose ONE):**
- Start ONE load of laundry (just start it - you'll move it later)
- OR: Move wet clothes to dryer and start it
- OR: Fold and put away ONE load that's been sitting
- OR: Gather all dirty laundry into hamper/basket (at least it's contained)

**Do not attempt more than one of these. Pick one. Do it. Done.**

**Estimated time:** 8m

---

#### **Step 10: The Reset Scan - Quick Visual Sweep**
**Description:**
Walk through your space once. Quick observations. One minute per room.

**Quick scan tasks:**
- Walk through each main room (30 seconds each)
- Notice: Does anything smell bad? (address it if yes)
- Notice: Is there any major safety issue? (address it if yes)
- Notice: Did you miss any obvious trash? (grab it)
- Notice: How do you feel about the space now vs. 90 minutes ago?

**That's it. Observation only. Don't start new tasks.**

**Estimated time:** 5m

---

#### **Step 11: Self-Care Checkpoint**
**Description:**
You just did something hard. Acknowledge it. This step is non-negotiable.

**Self-care tasks:**
- Get a glass of water. Drink it.
- Take a moment to sit down.
- Optional: Set a reminder for when to do this routine again (every 2-3 days is reasonable)
- Optional: Text someone you trust: "I cleaned today." You don't need praise, but acknowledgment is okay.
- Remember: You maintained your space even with low energy. That's strength.

**Estimated time:** 5m

---

### Important Notes for Low Energy Cleaning:

**Permission slips you need to hear:**
- ✅ This is enough. Really.
- ✅ You don't have to deep clean every time.
- ✅ Maintenance > perfection.
- ✅ Your space doesn't have to be Instagram-worthy to be valid.
- ✅ Doing this when you have low energy is an act of self-care.
- ✅ If you can only do 3 of these steps, that's still 3 more than zero.

**Sustainability strategy:**
- Do this routine every 2-3 days when in low energy mode
- It prevents the overwhelming buildup that makes depression worse
- 90 minutes twice a week is easier than 12 hours once a month
- Small maintenance prevents the emergency situations

**Emergency modifications (if even this is too much):**
If you truly cannot do all 11 steps, do ONLY these core 4:
1. Step 1: Kitchen basics (5m)
2. Step 2: Bathroom sanitize (10m)
3. Step 5: Disinfect high-touch surfaces (10m)
4. Step 7: Trash run (10m)

Total: 35 minutes. That's your absolute minimum for health and safety.

---

### Recovery Reminders:

You're not lazy. You're dealing with something real. The fact that you're even looking at this playbook means you're trying. That's not nothing. 

Some days, loading the dishwasher is a victory.
Some days, making the bed is an accomplishment.
Some days, taking out the trash takes everything you have.

All of those days are valid.
All of those efforts matter.

This playbook exists because maintaining your space maintains your health, even when - especially when - you're struggling. Use it without shame. Modify it without guilt. Do what you can.

You're doing better than you think you are.
