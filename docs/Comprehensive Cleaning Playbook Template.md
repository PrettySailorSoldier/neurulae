## ROOM-BASED DEEP CLEAN PLAYBOOKS

### Playbook 1: Kitchen Deep Clean Reset

**Category:** Cleaning

**Description:** Transform your kitchen from chaos to calm with this comprehensive deep clean. This playbook breaks down the overwhelming task of deep cleaning your kitchen into manageable, timed steps that help you stay focused and in control.

**Total Estimated Time:** 3h 30m

#### Steps:

**Step 1: Assemble Your Cleaning Arsenal**
- Gather all cleaning supplies in one location: all-purpose cleaner, degreaser, glass cleaner, microfiber cloths, scrub brushes, trash bags, and your favorite cleaning playlist
- This prevents mid-task supply hunting and keeps you in the zone
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 2: Clear & Declutter**
- Remove everything from countertops, tables, and visible surfaces
- Sort items: keep, relocate, discard
- A clear space is less overwhelming and faster to clean
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 3: Tackle the Dishes**
- Wash, dry, and put away all dishes
- Empty the dishwasher if needed, then load any dirty dishes
- Start the dishwasher so it runs while you clean other areas
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes

**Step 4: Deep Clean Major Appliances**
- **Refrigerator**: Remove all items, wipe shelves and drawers, clean exterior including handles and top, vacuum coils if accessible
- **Oven**: Apply oven cleaner, let sit while you work on other tasks (come back to it)
- **Microwave**: Heat bowl of water with lemon for 3 minutes, wipe interior, clean exterior
- **Dishwasher**: Wipe down exterior, clean around door seals
- **Stove/Range**: Remove grates and drip pans, soak in sink, wipe down stovetop and control panel
- **Estimated time:** 45 minutes

**Step 5: Clean Small Appliances**
- Wipe down toaster, coffee maker, blender, mixer, and any other countertop appliances
- Empty crumb trays, clean coffee pot with vinegar solution
- Polish stainless steel surfaces
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 6: Cabinet & Drawer Deep Dive**
- Empty one cabinet/drawer section at a time
- Wipe down interiors with all-purpose cleaner
- Check for expired items or things you no longer use
- Replace items in an organized manner
- Wipe down all cabinet fronts, handles, and knobs
- **Estimated time:** 30 minutes

**Step 7: Sink & Faucet Shine**
- Scrub sink with baking soda paste or specialized cleaner
- Use toothbrush for tight spots around the faucet
- Polish faucet until it shines
- Clean and disinfect the area around the sink
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 8: Countertop Perfection**
- Spray all countertops with appropriate cleaner
- Wipe down thoroughly, paying attention to corners and edges
- Clean behind small appliances
- Polish any special surfaces (granite, marble, etc.)
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 9: Walls, Windows & Light Fixtures**
- Spot clean any stains or grease splatters on walls
- Clean windows inside and out with glass cleaner
- Wipe down window sills and tracks
- Dust light fixtures and replace any burnt-out bulbs
- Wash or wipe down curtains/blinds
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes

**Step 10: Floor Finale**
- Sweep thoroughly, including corners and under appliances
- Move small appliances to clean underneath if possible
- Mop floor with appropriate cleaner for your floor type
- Let floor dry completely before replacing items
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes

**Step 11: Trash & Recycling Reset**
- Empty trash can, clean and disinfect interior and exterior
- Replace trash bag
- Organize recycling bins
- Take out all trash and recycling
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 12: Final Touch & Restock**
- Return all items to their clean spaces
- Arrange countertops intentionally with only essentials
- Replace dish towels with fresh ones
- Dispose of old sponges, replace if needed
- Stand back and admire your sparkling kitchen!
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

### Playbook 2: Bathroom Deep Clean Reset

**Category:** Cleaning

**Description:** A complete bathroom transformation system that tackles every surface, eliminates germs, and creates a spa-like sanctuary. Perfect for when you need a full bathroom refresh without feeling overwhelmed.

**Total Estimated Time:** 2h 20m

#### Steps:

**Step 1: Assemble Your Cleaning Toolkit**
- Gather all supplies: bathroom cleaner, disinfectant, glass cleaner, toilet bowl cleaner, scrub brushes, microfiber cloths, gloves, trash bags
- Having everything ready prevents distraction and keeps you focused
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 2: Clear All Surfaces**
- Remove everything from countertops, shelves, shower, and tub
- This gives you complete access and makes the space less overwhelming
- Sort items as you go: keep, toss, relocate
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 3: Dispose & Declutter**
- Check all products for expiration dates
- Discard empty bottles, old makeup, expired medications
- Remove items you no longer use
- A clearer bathroom is easier to maintain
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 4: Dust From Top to Bottom**
- Dust ceiling, light fixtures, and remove any cobwebs
- Wipe down exhaust fan/vent cover
- This prevents dust from falling on freshly cleaned surfaces
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 5: Tackle the Shower & Tub**
- Spray shower walls, tub, and fixtures with cleaner
- Let sit for 5-10 minutes while you work on other tasks
- Return and scrub thoroughly, paying attention to grout lines
- Remove and clean showerhead in vinegar solution if needed
- Scrub with brush for tough soap scum
- Rinse everything thoroughly
- Wash or replace shower curtain/liner if needed
- **Estimated time:** 30 minutes

**Step 6: Glass & Mirror Magic**
- Clean all glass doors with glass cleaner or vinegar solution
- Wipe down mirrors until streak-free and sparkling
- Don't forget mirror edges and frame
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 7: Sink & Faucet Deep Clean**
- Spray sink and faucet with cleaner
- Use toothbrush for crevices around the faucet
- Scrub sink bowl thoroughly
- Polish faucet until it shines
- Clean drain and ensure it's running freely
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 8: Counter & Cabinet Care**
- Wipe down all countertops thoroughly
- Clean under items as you replace them
- Wipe down cabinet exteriors and handles
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 9: Toilet Total Sanitization**
- Apply toilet bowl cleaner and let sit
- Wipe down exterior: tank, handle, base, and surrounding floor area
- Scrub inside of bowl thoroughly with toilet brush
- Clean and disinfect seat and lid - both sides
- Flush to rinse
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 10: Wall Wipe-Down**
- Wipe walls, especially around shower area
- Remove any soap scum, mildew, or water spots
- Pay attention to corners and edges
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 11: Floor Finish**
- Sweep or vacuum to remove hair and debris
- Pay special attention to corners and behind toilet
- Mop floor with appropriate cleaner
- Get into all the nooks and crannies
- **Estimated time:** 15 minutes

**Step 12: Air Vents & Fixtures**
- Remove and clean air vent covers
- Dust and vacuum inside vents if possible
- Wipe down light fixtures and replace burnt-out bulbs
- **Estimated time:** 10 minutes

**Step 13: Organize & Restock**
- Replace items neatly in their designated spots
- Organize under-sink cabinet
- Arrange countertop with only essentials
- Replace towels with fresh, clean ones
- Restock toilet paper and toiletries
- Add a nice-smelling hand soap or air freshener
- **Estimated time:** 20 minutes
